# 33 — Excess baggage fees and prepaid deadlines

## Scope and customer language

Retrieve for “extra bag fee,” “pay at the airport,” “cheaper online,” “overweight charge,” or “I will just pay later.” Fees below are handbook reference charges in US dollars for flights this carrier operates. Confirm the live fee table and the currency on the ticket before collecting payment.

## Required action

An extra piece paid before bag drop closes is 40 on a domestic ticket and 80 on an international ticket. The same piece paid at the airport is 75 domestic and 150 international. A checked piece above its included weight, up to 32 kg, is an overweight fee of 100 per bag. A piece above 158 cm and up to 203 cm is an oversize fee of 150. Quote both the prepaid and airport amounts, and say that the lower fee applies only when the payment is on the ticket or EMD before bag-drop closure.

If the passenger has not paid, offer payment now, a repack, or cargo for a bag the counter cannot accept. If the passenger refuses the fee and the bag is outside the allowance, the bag does not travel. Do not take cash outside the approved payment flow, and do not promise a refund of an airport fee because the online price was lower.

## Escalation and examples

Escalate a double charge, a fee taken for an included or prepaid piece, or a waiver request the agent cannot approve.

**Compliant:** “The second international piece is 80 if it is on the ticket before bag drop, and 150 at the airport. I can add it now, or you can travel with the included piece only.”

**Potentially non-compliant:** “Airport and online baggage prices are always the same.”

**Authority:** carrier fee table, ticket, and EMD record.
