# 39 — Undeclared dangerous goods and the passenger’s duty

## Scope and customer language

Retrieve for “fireworks,” “paint,” “petrol,” “gas canister,” “bleach,” “pepper spray,” “I did not know it was in the bag,” or “what is the fine.” The passenger must not pack hazardous goods in ordinary baggage, whether or not they are easy to see.

## Required action

Tell the passenger that explosives, fireworks, flares, fuel, paint, solvents, bleach, poisonous and infectious substances, and gas cylinders are forbidden in cabin and checked baggage. Self-defence spray is refused in the cabin. It may go in checked baggage only when both the carrier and the departure security authority allow that exact size, and only in the required container. The passenger must declare a doubtful item before bag drop. Not knowing that a canister was in the bag does not make the bag acceptable.

If the item is already packed, the passenger must remove it before check-in. Lawful alternatives are to leave it behind, surrender it to the airport process, or send it through an approved dangerous-goods shipper when that class of goods can legally move. Do not continue check-in on a bag the passenger says contains fuel, fireworks, or a leaking container. Undeclared dangerous goods can mean a refused bag, denied boarding, an airport or police report, and a civil or criminal penalty under the law of the airport state. Do not invent the fine amount and do not advise concealment.

## Escalation and examples

Escalate any item that is hot, leaking, smelling, or already inside a tagged bag. Use the safety escalation path rather than a baggage-fee conversation.

**Compliant:** “Fuel and paint cannot travel in your suitcase. Please take them out before we check the bag. I cannot quote a fine amount, but the airport can refuse the bag and report undeclared dangerous goods.”

**Potentially non-compliant:** “If it is a small can, leave it in the bag and say nothing.”

**Authority:** [FAA PackSafe](https://www.faa.gov/hazmat/packsafe); carrier dangerous-goods manual.
