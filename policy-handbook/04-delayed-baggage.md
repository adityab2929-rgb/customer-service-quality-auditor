# 04 — Delayed baggage and essential-expense claims

## Scope and evidence

Retrieve for “my bag did not arrive,” “bag still missing,” “necessities,” “delivery,” or “PIR/claim number.” Verify the bag tag, flight sectors, bag description, contact/delivery address, time reported, and tracking record. Ask the customer to report the missing bag promptly and provide the report reference; do not imply that a chat statement replaces the carrier’s tracing record.

## Required action

Open or locate the mishandled-baggage report, confirm contact details, explain the current trace status without inventing a location or delivery time, and provide the approved follow-up channel. Explain that reasonable, verifiable, actual incidental expenses caused by a delayed checked bag may be claimable under applicable rules and the carrier’s process; invite receipts and explain any local deadlines. Do not set an arbitrary daily spending cap unless the controlling published rule permits it.

For travel involving the United States, a carrier has responsibilities to locate the bag and to compensate eligible reasonable, verifiable incidental expenses, subject to liability limits. A paid checked-bag fee may need refunding when the delay becomes significant under the applicable rule. International carriage may be governed by the Montreal Convention and its current Special Drawing Right limit; route valuation questions to claims specialists.

## Escalation and examples

Escalate medical equipment, medication, an assistive device, a funeral/wedding/urgent welfare allegation, a lost passport, or a prolonged/no-trace case. Keep receipts, report number, and the agent’s promised follow-up in the record.

**Compliant:** “I’ve confirmed report ABC123. Please retain receipts for reasonable essentials while tracing continues; the claims team will assess them under the applicable baggage rules.”

**Potentially non-compliant:** “We never reimburse delayed-bag purchases.”

**Authority:** [US DOT delayed baggage guidance](https://www.transportation.gov/lost-delayed-or-damaged-baggage).
