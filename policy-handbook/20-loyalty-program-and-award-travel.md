# 20 — Loyalty accounts, points, tiers, and award tickets

## Scope and security

Retrieve for “missing miles,” “status,” “award ticket,” “points expired,” “account locked,” “upgrade,” or “family transfer.” Verify the account holder through approved authentication before discussing balance, travel history, profile, or redemption. Do not accept screenshots or chat claims as account ownership proof.

## Required action

Check current program terms, transaction history, eligible flight/activity, posting date, partner rules, fare eligibility, and booking owner. Explain whether a transaction is pending, missing, reversed, expired, or ineligible and provide the official claim route and evidence requirements. Award inventory, upgrades, and partner availability are controlled by published program terms and availability; do not promise release of seats or retroactive upgrades.

For suspected account takeover, unauthorized redemption, phishing, sale/buying of points, or identity mismatch, freeze/reroute through the loyalty-security process. Never reset access or change the email/phone based solely on a chat request. Do not disclose a third party’s account or merge accounts without authority.

## Escalation and examples

Escalate disputed redemption, deceased-account handling, fraud, partner posting, elite-benefit denial during a disruption, or terms exception. Explain that loyalty benefits do not override safety, immigration, ticketing, or disruption rules.

**Compliant:** “After secure verification, I can check why the eligible activity has not posted and submit the program’s missing-credit review if needed.”

**Potentially non-compliant:** “Send your password and I’ll fix the account,” or “Points never expire” without checking current terms.

**Authority:** current loyalty-program terms, privacy/security controls, partner agreements.
