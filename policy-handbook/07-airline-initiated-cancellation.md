# 07 — Airline-initiated cancellation and material schedule change

## Scope and classification

Retrieve for “the airline cancelled,” “schedule changed,” “my flight disappeared,” or “you moved me.” Confirm operating carrier, original and revised itinerary, notification time, reason recorded, whether travel began, and whether the passenger accepted a rebooking. Do not treat a voluntary change as airline cancellation without evidence.

## Required action

Clearly provide the available choices required by the applicable rule and contract: rebooking/rerouting, refund of the unused eligible portion, or other lawful option. Do not force a credit in place of a refund where a refund is owed. Give the customer reasonable information about the alternative transport, any service-assistance process, and how to choose. Obtain and log the customer’s selection; a choice may be binding under the applicable regime.

For EU Regulation 261/2004-covered cases, passengers affected by cancellation may have re-routing or reimbursement and assistance rights, and potential compensation subject to notice timing and extraordinary-circumstances analysis. Do not promise compensation: the operating carrier must assess facts and legal coverage. For US travel, DOT refund obligations can apply where the airline cancels or makes a significant change and the passenger declines the offered alternative.

## Escalation and examples

Escalate unaccompanied minors, accessibility needs, partner itineraries, visa/overnight issues, missed onward transport, disputed notice evidence, or compensation requests. Do not characterize weather, ATC, safety, or industrial action as qualifying/nonqualifying without operations evidence.

**Compliant:** “Because the airline cancelled the original flight, you can review the applicable rebooking and refund options before deciding; I will document your choice.”

**Potentially non-compliant:** “Only a voucher is possible after an airline cancellation.”

**Authority:** [EU Regulation 261/2004](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2004/261/oj/eng), [US DOT refund guidance](https://www.transportation.gov/individuals/aviation-consumer-protection/refunds).
