# 32 — Cabin bag and personal-item limits

## Scope and customer language

Retrieve for “carry-on size,” “personal item,” “under the seat,” “bag too big for the bin,” “gate check,” or “how heavy can my cabin bag be.” The cabin limit depends on the seat, not on how empty the flight looks.

## Required action

Economy and premium economy may take one cabin bag of 7 kg, no larger than 55 × 40 × 23 cm, plus one personal item no larger than 40 × 30 × 15 cm. Business and first may take one cabin bag of 10 kg within the same outer size, plus one personal item of the same personal-item size. The personal item must fit under the seat in front. It is not a second 7 kg cabin bag. Wheels, pockets, and handles count toward the size. The bag must also fit the airport sizer.

If the bag fails the sizer or the scale, the passenger may move items into the personal item, pay to send the bag as checked baggage when the checked-bag rule allows it, or leave the extra items behind with someone not travelling. A cabin bag that meets the limit can still be gate-checked at no extra fee when the bins are full. Before any gate check, the passenger must remove power banks, spare lithium batteries, and e-cigarettes and keep them in the cabin. A bag that misses the limit and is not paid or repacked is refused. Do not promise overhead space.

## Escalation and examples

Escalate a dispute at the gate, a bag already accepted online that fails the airport sizer, or a mobility device being sized as ordinary cabin baggage.

**Compliant:** “Premium economy still has a 7 kg cabin bag. If it does not fit the sizer, you can check it for the published fee or take out items until it fits.”

**Potentially non-compliant:** “The personal item can be another full-size suitcase.”

**Authority:** carrier cabin-baggage schedule and airport sizer rule.
