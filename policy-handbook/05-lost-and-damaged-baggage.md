# 05 — Lost, damaged, and missing-content baggage

## Scope and case separation

Retrieve for “bag declared lost,” “broken wheel,” “damaged suitcase,” “items missing,” or “compensation.” Distinguish delayed from lost, damage from normal wear, and damage that occurred while under the carrier’s control from pre-existing damage or inadequate packing. Do not declare a bag lost in chat unless the authorized baggage system has done so.

## Required action

For damage, request photos, bag tag, report reference, date/location discovered, purchase/repair evidence if available, and a clear description. Explain that the carrier will assess repair or reimbursement under the applicable contract and law; do not promise replacement at original purchase price. Normal wear may be excluded, but some jurisdictions restrict exclusions for components such as wheels, handles, and straps.

For a bag officially declared lost, send the prescribed inventory/claim workflow and explain that valuation, depreciation, exclusions, and liability limits apply. International itineraries can be governed by the Montreal Convention; domestic rules can be different. Do not say that electronics, valuables, cash, or fragile items are always excluded—check the applicable contract and international treaty context. Refund any bag fee only under the relevant rule and process.

## Escalation and examples

Escalate assistive-device damage, a high-value/cross-border claim, alleged theft or tampering, a customer refusing settlement, or a statutory deadline. Preserve all photos and exact promises.

**Compliant:** “The bag has not yet been declared lost. I can start the tracing/claim process and explain the evidence needed if its status changes.”

**Potentially non-compliant:** “International bags are never covered for electronics,” or “A scuff always earns a new suitcase.”

**Authority:** [US DOT lost/damaged baggage guidance](https://www.transportation.gov/lost-delayed-or-damaged-baggage), [Montreal Convention overview](https://www.icao.int/secretariat/legal/List%20of%20Parties/Mtl99_EN.pdf).
