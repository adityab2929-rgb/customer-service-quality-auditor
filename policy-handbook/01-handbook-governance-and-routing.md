# 01 — Handbook governance and case routing

**Purpose.** This is an internal decision-support handbook for quality review and customer support. It is not the airline’s contract of carriage, tariff, fare rule, safety manual, or legal advice. For every case, the controlling source is the published contract and fare conditions applicable to the ticket, the operating carrier’s rules, and mandatory law at the itinerary’s origin, destination, and transit points. Do not promise an outcome before checking those sources.

## Retrieval terms and case facts

Retrieve this file for: “what am I entitled to,” “your policy,” “agent promised,” “supervisor,” “complaint,” or any case crossing jurisdictions. Record itinerary, ticketing and operating carrier, flight number/date, departure and arrival points, notification time, cause shown in operations notes, fare/ancillary purchased, payment method, and exact customer request. Preserve chat timestamps and the agent’s exact words.

## Required support response

State what is known, what will be checked, and the next owner or deadline. Separate a legal or contractual entitlement from a goodwill request. Do not create an exception, waive a safety rule, alter a ticket, or disclose another passenger’s data without authorized workflow. Use the customer’s preferred accessible channel where available.

Escalate to a trained specialist when a rule conflicts, a regulator/court/lawyer is mentioned, the itinerary includes multiple carriers or a package/agency booking, or the customer alleges discrimination, injury, fraud, safety, or a privacy incident. Quality reviewers should mark “insufficient evidence” rather than infer facts absent from the record.

## Compliance examples

**Compliant:** “I can see the cancellation notice. I’ll verify which carrier operated the flight and the options under your ticket and applicable passenger-rights rules before I confirm a refund or rebooking.”

**Potentially non-compliant:** “Every delayed flight gets cash compensation,” or “Our policy always overrides local law.”

**Sources to consult:** [US DOT consumer guidance](https://www.transportation.gov/airconsumer), [EU air passenger rights](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/passenger-rights/air/index_en.htm).
