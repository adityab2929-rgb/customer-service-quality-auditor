# 40 — Passport validity and name match

## Scope and customer language

Retrieve for “passport expiry,” “six months,” “blank pages,” “name does not match,” “middle name missing,” or “can I fly on an ID card.” The passenger must hold a document that matches the ticket and satisfies every country on the journey.

## Required action

Compare the travel-document surname and given names with the ticket before promising travel. A domestic journey may allow the identity document this carrier publishes for that country. An international journey needs a passport, or another document border authorities at origin, transit, and destination accept for that nationality. Many states require the passport to remain valid for six months after the date of exit and to have blank pages. Some accept a shorter validity. Read the current rule for the actual itinerary. Do not treat “six months” as universal, and do not tell a passenger to travel on a damaged, cancelled, or expired passport.

If the passport is too short, full, or not the right document, the alternatives are to renew or replace it, change to a destination whose current rule accepts it, or postpone. A name that does not match needs the name-correction process before departure, not an explanation at the gate. The likely result of travelling anyway is denial at check-in or refusal at the border. The airline does not pay compensation for a document the passenger failed to hold, and the fare follows the ticket’s no-show or refund rule. Never suggest altering a passport.

## Escalation and examples

Escalate a same-day departure, a passenger already denied by document check, a minor, or a dual national using the wrong passport.

**Compliant:** “The ticket says Lopez Garcia and the passport says Lopez. I need to correct the ticket to the passport name before travel. I cannot confirm that a passport expiring next month meets the destination’s validity rule.”

**Potentially non-compliant:** “An expired passport is fine when the visa inside it is still valid.”

**Authority:** destination and transit entry rules; carrier travel-document tool.
