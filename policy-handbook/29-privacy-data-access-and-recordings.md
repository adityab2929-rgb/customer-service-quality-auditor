# 29 — Privacy, personal data, recordings, and chat records

## Scope and protected information

Retrieve for “delete my data,” “send recording,” “who can see my booking,” “GDPR,” “privacy,” “recording,” or a request about another passenger. Booking, payment, passport, health/accessibility, loyalty, and chat data may be personal or sensitive information. Authenticate before disclosing account-specific information.

## Required action

Collect the minimum information needed to service the case and use approved systems only. Do not paste passport, payment, medical, or security information into unsecured chat or notes. Verify a requester’s authority before discussing another passenger, minor, employee, or corporate traveler. Do not release call recordings, staff details, CCTV, manifests, or internal case notes outside the approved privacy/legal process.

Route access, correction, deletion, objection, retention, and marketing-preference requests to the official privacy workflow. Explain that deletion/access rights may have legal exceptions and identity verification requirements; do not promise immediate deletion. Preserve records under applicable legal hold, safety, complaint, and audit requirements.

## Escalation and examples

Escalate suspected data breach, misdirected email/document, account takeover, government/law-enforcement request, media request, child data, health data, or a customer asserting statutory privacy rights. Contain and report promptly; do not try to “fix” a breach by deleting evidence.

**Compliant:** “I can help route your data-access request through our privacy team. For security, I cannot send booking or recording details until the approved identity verification is complete.”

**Potentially non-compliant:** “I’ll email you everyone on the booking’s passport details.”

**Authority:** applicable privacy law, privacy notice, retention schedule, security incident procedure.
