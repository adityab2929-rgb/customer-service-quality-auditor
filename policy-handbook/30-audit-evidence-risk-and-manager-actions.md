# 30 — Quality-audit evidence, risk rating, and coaching actions

## Review standard

Use this file when an AI or manager reviews a completed chat. Assess the conversation against the policy retrieved for the actual issue, not against an ideal answer invented after the fact. Quote the exact customer and agent messages, link the specific policy passage, and distinguish confirmed facts from assumptions. A polite tone does not cure incorrect advice; an incomplete chat is not proof of misconduct.

## Suggested risk model

**High risk:** safety/security mishandling; wrong advice that could cause immediate loss of travel, money, accessibility, privacy, or legal rights; refusal of mandatory process; discriminatory/retaliatory language; unauthorized data/payment handling; invented policy; unlogged urgent escalation. Manager action: urgent containment, specialist review, customer remediation assessment, and targeted coaching or incident process.

**Medium risk:** material policy ambiguity, missed verification, premature promise/denial, incorrect routing, inaccurate timeline, or failure to disclose an important condition—where harm is possible but can likely be corrected. Manager action: correct the customer record promptly, review outcome, coach with the exact policy section, and monitor recurrence.

**Low risk:** minor clarity/tone/documentation gap without a material wrong outcome. Manager action: feedback, template improvement, and sample-based follow-up.

## Required report fields and examples

Record issue; sentiment; escalation risk; compliance status (compliant, partial, non-compliant, insufficient evidence); concise summary; exact supporting chat quote; retrieved policy title/section; and a specific next action. Do not use sentiment as a credibility score. Example: “Agent said ‘all delayed flights get cash’; Policy 08 requires eligibility review. **Medium/High** depending on customer reliance and jurisdiction. Action: correct statement and assess claim.”

**Quality guardrails:** never infer protected traits, never fabricate a policy citation, and flag uncertain retrieval for human review. Aggregate dashboards should report counts separately from confirmed breaches.
