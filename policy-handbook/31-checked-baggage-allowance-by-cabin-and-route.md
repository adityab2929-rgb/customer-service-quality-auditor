# 31 — Checked baggage allowance by cabin and route

## Scope and customer language

Retrieve for “how many kilos,” “checked bag weight,” “economy versus business,” “long-haul allowance,” “infant bag,” or “what does my seat include.” These are this carrier’s handbook reference limits for flights it operates. Quote the allowance printed on the ticket when it differs. A partner-operated flight can follow the operating carrier’s rule.

## Required action

State the allowance in pieces, kilos, and total linear centimeters, and say which cabin and route produced that figure. Economy Light includes no checked bag on any route. Domestic economy, and international economy on a flight under six hours, includes one piece at 23 kg and 158 cm. International economy on a flight of six hours or longer includes two pieces, each at 23 kg and 158 cm. Premium economy includes two pieces, each at 23 kg and 158 cm. Business includes two pieces, each at 32 kg and 158 cm. First includes three pieces, each at 32 kg and 158 cm. An infant under two without a paid seat includes one piece at 10 kg, plus one fully collapsible stroller or one approved car seat, in addition to the accompanying adult’s allowance. A loyalty benefit or prepaid bag counts only when the ticket or EMD shows it.

If the passenger’s bag is above that allowance, offer a prepaid extra piece or overweight fee before bag drop, a repack into another included piece, or carriage as cargo. If the passenger keeps a non-accepted bag, bag drop must refuse it. Do not promise that airport staff will waive the limit.

## Escalation and examples

Escalate a partner airline, a mismatched ticket allowance, a paid bag that does not appear on the EMD, or a medical or mobility item being treated as ordinary baggage.

**Compliant:** “Your international economy ticket is under six hours, so the handbook allowance is one checked piece at 23 kg. I will compare that with the allowance printed on the ticket before you pay for a second piece.”

**Potentially non-compliant:** “Every economy ticket includes two bags of 32 kg.”

**Authority:** ticket and fare brand; operating-carrier baggage table; [US DOT baggage guidance](https://www.transportation.gov/lost-delayed-or-damaged-baggage).
