# 08 — Flight delay, care, and customer communication

## Scope and records

Retrieve for “flight delayed,” “stuck on plane,” “missed event,” “hotel/meal,” or “compensation.” Record original and revised times, location, operating carrier, reason as logged by operations, notification timestamps, customer’s onward connection, and any assistance already offered. Do not speculate on a cause or promise a departure time that operations has not confirmed.

## Required action

Give timely, factual updates with the source and next update point. Check the relevant disruption matrix for rebooking, meals, accommodation, ground transport, communication, and refund eligibility; distinguish legal entitlement from discretionary goodwill. If delay becomes cancellation or the passenger declines a significant airline-initiated change, route to the correct policy rather than keeping it as a simple delay.

For EU261-covered delays, assistance thresholds depend on flight distance and expected delay; long-delay reimbursement and compensation rules need case-specific assessment. For US domestic travel, general federal rules do not create a universal delay-compensation entitlement; commitments may arise from the carrier’s customer service plan or contract. Onboard/tarmac delay reports must go immediately to operations compliance; never instruct a passenger to leave an aircraft or disregard crew directions.

## Escalation and examples

Escalate medical/welfare needs, stranded minors, missed protected connections, mass disruption, alleged discrimination, or tarmac-delay concerns. Preserve the exact update and whether the customer accepted rebooking.

**Compliant:** “The aircraft is delayed; I’m checking the operating carrier’s recorded cause and your applicable care/rebooking options. I cannot confirm compensation until that review is complete.”

**Potentially non-compliant:** “Any delay over two hours automatically gets cash.”

**Authority:** [EU passenger rights](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/passenger-rights/air/index_en.htm), carrier customer-service plan.
